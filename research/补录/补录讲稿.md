# 补充录音稿：讲课里的英文句子和各种句式

## 为什么要录这一份

之前的录音里，完整的英文句子很少，大多是夹在中文里的单词和短语；提问、强调、列举这几种句子也不多。这份稿子就是专门补这几块的，让声音模型学会你平时怎么读英文例句，怎么提问，怎么强调，怎么一项一项地列举。

## 怎么录

用平时录课的那支麦克风，在同一个房间里录，嘴和麦克风的距离也和平时讲课一样。

房间要安静：关上门窗，关掉空调和风扇，手机调成静音。不要放音乐，也不要开任何背景声。

就像平时给学生上课那样去讲：语速正常，该停顿的地方自然停顿，该强调的地方照你平时的习惯去强调，不要像念课文那样一字一字地读。英文句子也一样，就像平时在课上给学生读例句那样。

每一行前面的数字只是编号，方便以后把录音和文字对上，不用读出来。

尽量照着稿子读，不要临时加字或者减字。

每读完一行，停大约一秒钟，再读下一行。

如果哪一行读错了，或者读得不满意，不用停下录音，也不用删，稍微停一下，从这一行的开头重新读一遍就可以了。

录音存成WAV格式。每个部分存一个文件就可以，文件名就用这个部分的名字，比如“定语从句.wav”。读累了可以随时停下来休息，休息完另开一个文件接着读也没关系，文件名后面加上“2”就行。

预计时长：全文一共243行，其中有151句完整的英文句子。只算朗读的话大约25分钟，加上每行之间的停顿大约30分钟；算上重读和休息，留出一个小时左右会比较从容。这个时间是按字数估算的，实际会因人而异。

## 定语从句

文件名：定语从句.wav

1. 从句主要可以分为三大类，定语从句、名词性从句和状语从句。好，那我们就先从定语从句开始。
2. A relative clause usually comes right after the noun it describes.
3. The farmer who owns these fields holds a meeting every autumn.
4. The world is full of curious people who love to learn.
5. 也就是说，定语从句的作用就相当于一个形容词，用来修饰和限定前面的那个先行词。

6. 好，我们来看一下下面这个句子，The girl who sits next to me is my best friend.
7. Frank is a basketball player whom I really like.
8. She is a doctor whom everyone in the town respects.
9. The teacher whom we all respect has retired.
10. who可以做主语，也可以做宾语，但是whom只能做宾语，比如The man who called me yesterday is my uncle，这里的who做的就是主语。

11. 那如果先行词是物的话，我们一般就要用which或者that，大家看这几个句子。
12. This is the phone which Peter bought last month.
13. The book that you lent me last week was really helpful.
14. The worlds that she builds in her novels feel completely real.
15. She accepts gifts only from the friends she trusts.
16. 在这几个句子里面，关系代词在从句中做的都是宾语，所以说我们也可以把它省略掉，就像后面这个句子一样。

17. 好，那接下来我们来看一下whose，大家先看这几个句子。
18. I know a girl whose brother plays the violin very well.
19. The artist whose paintings hang in the hall lives in a quiet village.
20. The house whose windows are broken is very old.
21. 一定要注意，whose后面必须紧跟一个名词，比如在The house whose windows are broken这个句子里面，whose修饰的就是windows。

22. 如果先行词里面既有人又有物，那关系代词要用哪一个呢？比如He talked about the people and things that he saw in the village.
23. The old lady and her dog that live next door are very friendly.
24. This is the very book that I wanted.
25. This is the twelfth time that I've asked you to close the window.
26. The town is no longer the place that it was twenty years ago.
27. 所以说，当先行词里面既有人又有物，或者被the very和序数词修饰，又或者关系代词在从句里面做表语的时候，我们一般只能用that。

28. 那接下来我们来看一下非限定性定语从句，它和限定性定语从句到底有什么区别呢？
29. My sister, who lives in the city, is a nurse.
30. My father, who works in a hospital, gets up very early every day.
31. They called Jason a hero, which he really was.
32. 限定性定语从句对先行词有限定作用，而非限定性定语从句只是补充说明。大家一定要记住，非限定性定语从句不能用that来引导。

33. 那问题来了，什么时候用that，什么时候用which呢？
34. That, which, who, whom, and whose are all relative pronouns.
35. The bike that I bought last year is already broken.
36. My bike, which I bought last year, is already broken.
37. 要判断用that还是which，首先看有没有逗号，其次看前面有没有介词，然后再看先行词是什么。

38. 好，那我们再来看一下介词加关系代词的情况。
39. The man with whom she was talking is our new neighbor.
40. This is the house in which he lived.
41. The person to whom I spoke was very kind.
42. 这个规则特别重要，介词加关系代词的时候，只能用which或者whom，不能用that。

43. 大家看一下下面这两个句子，它们有什么不一样呢？
44. This is the person whom Lucy is looking for.
45. This is the person for whom Lucy is looking.
46. 其实这两种写法的意思是完全一样的，for whom Lucy is looking和whom Lucy is looking for没有任何区别。只不过在比较正式的写作中，我们一般会把介词提到前面。

47. 关系副词主要有三个，when、where和why，它们一般可以换成介词加which的形式，比如on which、in which和for which。
48. Sunday is the day when I can rest at home.
49. This is the village where my grandmother was born.
50. That is the reason why he left the party so early.
51. Is this the house where you were born?
52. 一定要注意，when作为关系副词是可以换成on which的，所以我们也可以说Sunday is the day on which I can rest at home.

53. 接下来我们来看why，这个词很有意思，为什么呢？因为它修饰的先行词基本上只有一个，那就是reason。
54. The reason why he was late is that his alarm clock didn't ring.
55. I still don't know the reason why she changed her mind.
56. 那是不是只要先行词是reason，就一定要用why呢？不是的。大家看这个句子，This is the reason that he gave me，这里的that在从句里面做了宾语，所以就不能用why。

57. 那大家看一下我们平时常说的这句话，Where there is a will, there is a way. 这里的where引导的还是定语从句吗？
58. Put the book back where you found it.
59. We sat down where we could see the stage clearly.
60. 其实不是的，这几个句子里面where前面都没有先行词，所以它们引导的都是状语从句。只有像This is the park where we often play football这样，where修饰前面的名词park，它引导的才是定语从句。

61. 接下来我们来学习另外一个关系代词as，这个关系代词相对来说比较特殊。
62. As is reported, the number of smokers has dropped by fifty percent in just one year.
63. As you can see, the plan worked very well.
64. 这里大家要特别注意，as引导的定语从句可以放在句首，比如As we all know, practice makes perfect.

65. 那as和which到底有什么区别呢？我们来看下面这几个句子。
66. As I expected, he passed the exam.
67. As we expected, the team won the game.
68. He passed the test, which surprised everyone in the class.
69. 首先，as可以代替整个主句；其次，它表达的是一种期待和预期。但是which所引导的定语从句只是在陈述一个事实，并没有这种期待。

70. 大家看一下这个句子，That boy who is talking is Jim. 是不是出现了两个is？那哪一个才是主句的谓语呢？
71. Now let's take a look at this sentence together.
72. Although he had studied for many hours, he still could not remember the rules that the teacher had explained in class.
73. The boy enjoyed the noisy toy that his uncle gave him.
74. 大家一定要先把主干找出来，比如The man who lives next door is a pilot，主干就是The man is a pilot.

## 名词性从句

文件名：名词性从句.wav

75. 了解了定语从句之后，我们接下来就要学习名词性从句了，比如I believe that he is honest.
76. In this sentence, the clause works as the object of the verb.
77. It is clear that this clause is the subject of the sentence.
78. 名词性从句主要可以做四种成分：主语、宾语、表语和同位语，比如What he said is true.

79. 如果我们直接说smoking kills is a well-known fact，这个句子里面一共出现了两个谓语，一个是kills，一个是is，所以这个句子是错的。
80. That smoking kills is a well-known fact.
81. We all know that smoking kills.
82. 所以说，我们一定要在smoking kills前面加上that，把它变成一个名词性从句，这样它才能放在主语的位置上。

83. 那这个时候我们还能省略that吗？The fact that smoking kills is known to everyone. 这里的that能不能去掉？
84. The news that we are having a holiday tomorrow is not true.
85. His suggestion that we should take turns cleaning the classroom was accepted.
86. 说白了，同位语从句就是对前面那个抽象名词的解释，比如The idea that he might quit his job worried his mother.

87. 那名词性从句里面的that，什么时候可以省略呢？
88. I hope that you will enjoy the trip.
89. The problem is that nobody remembered the address.
90. 在口语中我们经常省略宾语从句里面的that，但是在比较正式的书面写作里面，大家还是要把that保留下来，比如He said that he would call me back later.

91. 那问题来了，Does smoking kill这个一般疑问句，我们要怎么把它变成名词性从句呢？
92. Whether smoking kills or not is no longer a question.
93. Nobody knows whether he will come back next year.
94. The question is whether we have enough time.
95. I wonder whether the shop is still open.
96. 大家一定要注意，whether经常和or not搭配在一起用，比如I don't know whether he will come or not.

97. 好，讲完了whether，那我们再来看一下if，Ask him if he needs any help.
98. I wonder if she will come to the party that we are having next Saturday evening.
99. It depends on whether he agrees.
100. Whether can be followed by or not, but if usually cannot.
101. 所以说，if引导的名词性从句只能做动词的宾语。大家千万不要把if和whether搞混，在介词后面只能用whether。

102. 好，那我们现在进入下一个部分，看一下what这个连接代词，I know what happened last night.
103. I wonder what he bought.
104. What he learned is valuable.
105. 所以说，what he learned它的本质并不是句子，而是一个具有句子结构的名词。

106. 好，我们来看一个场景，Jason走进一家酒吧，酒保看到他之后说了这么一句话，I know what you want.
107. What I want to buy is a new book, a box of chocolates, and some coffee.
108. In this clause, what stands for everything that I want to buy.
109. 也就是说，what在这里扮演的就是一个集合，What I need now is a good night's sleep.

110. 大家想一想，Tell me what you saw这个句子里面，what到底做了从句的什么成分呢？
111. I know that he is right.
112. I know what he wants.
113. In a noun clause, what must play a role, but that does not.
114. 所以说，that在名词性从句里面不做任何成分，而what必须在从句里面做成分。

115. 好，那接下来我们来看一下whoever和whatever。
116. Whoever comes early will get a small prize.
117. Whoever needs help can come to my office after class.
118. Whatever you decide, I will support you.
119. 在前面两个句子里面，whoever都做了从句的主语，表示无论是谁。而在后面这个句子里面，whatever引导的其实是一个状语从句，表示无论你做出什么样的决定。

120. 接下来我们换一个角度，从翻译的方向来看一下这个句子，I don't know who broke the window.
121. How she solved the problem is still a mystery.
122. Why he refused the offer is hard to understand.
123. 在翻译这种句子的时候，我们要先把名词性从句翻译出来，然后再依次翻译出句子的谓语和其他成分。

124. 比如我们在路上问路的时候，可能就会说这么一句话，Could you tell me where the station is?
125. Do you know where the hair dryer is?
126. Could you tell me whether the meeting has been cancelled?
127. Pay attention to the position of the verb in this clause.
128. 那Do you know where she lives这个句子里面，为什么where后面不用倒装呢？因为在名词性从句里面，我们要用陈述句的语序。

## 并列连词

文件名：并列连词.wav

129. 并列连词主要有这么几个，and、but、or、nor、so和yet，大家一定要把它们记熟。
130. The rain stopped, and a rainbow appeared over the hills.
131. Tony is smart, but he is lazy.
132. 主从复合句里面的从句没有办法单独存在，但是并列句里面的两个句子都可以单独存在，比如I like tea, and my brother likes coffee.

133. 我们先来看and，就比如说Tom and Jerry are good friends，这里的and把两个主语连在了一起。
134. At the market, my mother bought bananas, grapes, and oranges.
135. We need paper, pencils, rulers, and glue for the art class.
136. and可以把三个甚至更多的同类成分连在一起，比如My sister likes singing, dancing, and drawing.

137. 好，那接下来我们就来看一下另外一个连词but，我们先来看这个例句，I called him twice, but nobody answered the phone.
138. She bought apples and bread, but she forgot the milk.
139. She looks quiet, but she is actually very funny.
140. It was raining, but she still went shopping.
141. 按理说下雨天并不适合出门购物，但是我们用but进行了一个转折，所以说but前后的两个句子，意思上是有转折的。

142. 好，关于and和but的用法我们就讲到这里，下面我们来学习or，Would you like tea or coffee?
143. Would you like to sit by the window or near the door?
144. Shall we meet at the library or at the coffee shop?
145. Take an umbrella, or you will get wet.
146. 首先，or可以连接两个选择；其次，在否定句里面，我们一般用or来代替and，比如I don't smoke or drink.

147. 大家想一想，I cannot sing, nor can I dance. 这个句子里面，为什么要把can提到I的前面呢？
148. I can neither sing nor dance.
149. She didn't call, nor did she send me a message.
150. 为什么呢？因为nor本身就带有否定的意思，当它放在句子开头的时候，后面的句子就要用倒装。

151. 大家看这个句子，Either you or he is right. 那谓语动词is是跟着you走，还是跟着he走呢？
152. Either you or your brother will be chosen as team leader.
153. Neither the students nor the teacher was aware of the change.
154. 一定要注意，Neither the teacher nor the students were late. 这里的谓语动词要和靠近它的那个主语保持一致，也就是说，主谓一致要遵循就近原则。

155. 那接下来我们来看一下so。
156. I missed the last train, so I had to take a taxi.
157. It was getting late, so we decided to stay at a hotel.
158. 这一点大家一定要记住，so前后连接的两个句子要用逗号隔开，比如The road was icy, so we drove very slowly.

159. 好，那我们再来看一下yet。
160. He worked very hard, yet he failed the exam.
161. He has plenty of money, yet he lives a very simple life.
162. The question looked easy, yet nobody in the class could answer it.
163. 一定要注意，yet在这里不是还没有的意思，而是表示转折，比如She is young, yet she knows a lot.

164. 除了这几个并列连词以外，还有一些成对出现的连词，大家看下面这两个句子。
165. Not only did he finish his own work, but he also helped the others.
166. Both the singer and the dancer were nervous before the show.
167. 大家一定要注意，not only放在句子开头的时候，后面的句子要用倒装，所以这里我们说的是did he finish。

## 句型、时态、问句、感叹句和数字

文件名：句型和问句.wav

168. 那我们就先从系动词开始，这个句子里面的系动词就是is，The answer is very simple.
169. He is a teacher.
170. He works as a teacher.
171. 大家觉得He is a teacher和He works as a teacher这两个句子有什么区别呢？

172. 大家想一想，The book is on the desk属于五大基本句型里面的哪一种句型呢？
173. My brother plays the guitar.
174. My mother gave me a new bike.
175. 首先看主语，然后看谓语，接着再看宾语，Lucy is looking for that person这个句子就非常清楚了。

176. 好，那我们再来看几个时态比较复杂的句子，大家注意一下动词的形式。
177. By the time we arrived, the movie had already started.
178. She was reading a novel when the lights suddenly went out.
179. We will have finished the project by next Friday.
180. He has been learning to play the guitar since last spring.
181. 也就是说，同样是一个动作，发生的时间不一样，动词的形式也就跟着不一样。

182. 好，下面这几个问题，大家可以先自己想一想。
183. Which word is the subject of this sentence?
184. Can you find the clause in this sentence?
185. Why is this clause called a noun clause?
186. How long have you been learning grammar?
187. 那这个时候大家先不要急着看答案，先自己把句子的主干找出来。

188. 那我们再来看几个生活中经常会用到的疑问句。
189. Do you have any brothers or sisters?
190. Have you ever been to the mountains in winter?
191. Where did you grow up?
192. What do you usually do on weekends?
193. 前面两个是一般疑问句，可以用yes或者no来回答，而后面两个是特殊疑问句，就没有办法用yes或者no来回答了。

194. 好，接下来我们再看几个语气不太一样的问句。
195. Why didn't you tell me the truth?
196. Isn't it strange that nobody noticed the mistake?
197. Are you sure the tour starts at noon?
198. Did you finish your homework last night?
199. 也就是说，同样是问句，说话的人带着的情绪不一样，句子的语气也会很不一样。

200. 那接下来我们来看一下感叹句，感叹句主要有两种，一种是用what，一种是用how。
201. What a beautiful day it is!
202. How quickly the children grow up!
203. What a clever idea that was!
204. How lucky we are to have such good friends!
205. 大家一定要注意，what后面跟的是名词，而how后面跟的是形容词或者副词，比如How strange the weather has been this week!

206. 周末你是想去爬山、打篮球，还是宅在家里呢？Will you go hiking, play basketball, or stay home this weekend?
207. She washed her face, brushed her teeth, and went to bed.
208. Tomatoes, eggs, onions, and a little salt are all you need.
209. We visited the museum, the park, and the zoo.
210. Read the sentence, find the clause, and mark the subject.
211. 在We visited这个句子里面，我们用and连接了三个地点，它们都做了谓语动词visited的宾语。

212. 好，那我们再来看几个带数字的句子，大家注意一下这些数字的读法。
213. Jason was born on the fifth of June, two thousand and three.
214. Peter has lived in this town since nineteen ninety-nine.
215. Tony's birthday is on the twenty-first of October.
216. The meeting starts at half past nine on Thursday morning.
217. 大家可以看到，像nineteen ninety-nine这样的年份，一般是两位两位地读，而日期要用序数词来读，比如the fifth和the twenty-first。

218. 我们再来看几个和时间、数量有关的句子。
219. About sixty percent of the students passed the exam.
220. There are three hundred and sixty-five days in a year.
221. Frank scored twenty-eight points in last night's game.
222. Lucy, Jason, and Tony will meet at eight o'clock on Saturday.
223. 大家可以数一下这个句子有几个单词，I have two brothers and one sister，一共是七个单词。
